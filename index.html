Mall Tycoon

Build the busiest mall in town

How to Play

  • 🏬 Open stores — Tap a FOR LEASE space and pick a store to open there. You start with $150. Stores come in six families: Dining, Café, Fashion, Tech, Books & Play and Health (Health and the Toy Store unlock as you earn).
  • 🚶 Shoppers — Each shopper shows want icons (🍽️ ☕ 👗 💻 📚 🏥) and has a budget. They walk to a store that sells what they want at a price they can afford. If your mall doesn't sell it, they leave unhappy, so open a mix of families.
  • 🧾 Registers & lines — Every sale happens at a checkout register. Shoppers line up inside the store and the front of the line takes the next free register. A new store has 1 register.
  • ⬆️ Store upgrades — Tap a store: 📦 Goods (+10% price per sale), ⏱️ Service (faster checkout) and 👥 Capacity (+1 register). Each upgrade raises the store's level, and levels 5, 10, 15 and 20 give a free extra register (6 max).
  • 🛒 Shop & Upgrades — Mall-wide boosts: Moving Walkways (faster shoppers), Marketing (more profit) and Advertising (more shoppers). Once every space is filled, Expand Mall for new spaces. Earning more unlocks tier 2 and 3 stores.
  • 💰 Earning cash — Each sale pays the store's price. Spend it on new stores and upgrades. While you're away the mall earns 50% of its income rate, for up to 8 hours. Claim daily goals for bonuses. A full level-10 mall can Prestige for stars and perks.
